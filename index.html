Our Roasting Philosophy

A Sanctuary Dedicated to
Slow Living & Pure Flavours

Demo Café was conceived as a quiet rebellion against rushed mornings. We believe that honest hospitality begins with respecting every link of the craft — from the soil of direct-trade farms to the temperature of each pour.

Every green bean lot is roasted in small 5kg batches to preserve its natural origin profile. We never use artificial syrups or over-roast, letting notes of jasmine, bergamot, stone fruits, and dark cacao speak for themselves.
